## What changed

Standalone Instant now provides a route back to the demo projects and workspace setup.

## What to do

Update Windows to [Preview 2](https://github.com/FerMatter/fermatter.github.io/releases/tag/v0.11.0-windows-preview.2) or later. For macOS and Ubuntu, install Preview 5 or later from [Download](https://fermatter.github.io/download/). See [Instant](https://fermatter.github.io/guide/paper-to-slides/#instant).
