## What changed

A scan that completes late no longer replaces a newer Library edit.

## What to do

The fix is already in [macOS Preview 1](https://github.com/FerMatter/fermatter.github.io/releases/tag/v0.11.0-preview.1) and later. See [Library variants](https://fermatter.github.io/guide/paper-to-slides/).
