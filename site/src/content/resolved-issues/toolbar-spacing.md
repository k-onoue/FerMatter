## What changed

The PDF, Library and Source headers no longer repeat information already shown by the interface. Window controls are separated from project actions.

## What to do

Update to [macOS Preview 4](https://github.com/FerMatter/fermatter.github.io/releases/tag/v0.11.0-preview.4) or later. Further [Library/Source icon alignment](https://fermatter.github.io/resolved-issues/toolbar-alignment/) is tracked separately.
