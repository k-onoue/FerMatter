## What changed

Renaming or moving source files retains the associations needed to recover drafts and keep the selected main file.

## What to do

The fix is already in [macOS Preview 1](https://github.com/FerMatter/fermatter.github.io/releases/tag/v0.11.0-preview.1) and later. See [Source editing](https://fermatter.github.io/guide/paper-to-slides/).
