## What changed

Invalid metadata is kept for recovery rather than replaced by an empty project.

## What to do

The fix is already in [macOS Preview 1](https://github.com/FerMatter/fermatter.github.io/releases/tag/v0.11.0-preview.1) and later. See [project setup](https://fermatter.github.io/guide/).
