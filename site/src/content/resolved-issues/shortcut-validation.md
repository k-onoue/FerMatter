## What changed

Custom shortcuts and Ctrl+Tab navigation are preserved when settings are saved or reloaded.

## What to do

Update to [Windows Preview 3](https://github.com/FerMatter/fermatter.github.io/releases/tag/v0.11.0-windows-preview.3) or later. See [keyboard shortcuts](https://fermatter.github.io/guide/shortcuts/).
