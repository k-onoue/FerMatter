## What changed

This addresses sharing errors involving a settings backup during rendering or export.

## What to do

The fix is in [Windows Preview 2](https://github.com/FerMatter/fermatter.github.io/releases/tag/v0.11.0-windows-preview.2) and later. The macOS and Ubuntu packages still await this fix; reinstalling their current downloads may not resolve it.
