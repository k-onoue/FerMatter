## What changed

Previously, opening one card’s context menu could export another selected card.

## What to do

Update Windows to [Preview 2](https://github.com/FerMatter/fermatter.github.io/releases/tag/v0.11.0-windows-preview.2) or later. macOS and Ubuntu include this fix in Preview 5 and later. See [export formats](https://fermatter.github.io/guide/export/).
