\section{A few useful building blocks}
These unrelated textbook identities complete the demonstration library.
They exercise piecewise definitions, sums, and multiline equations.
\begin{equation}
  h(x)=\begin{cases}
    0, & x<0,\\
    x, & x\geq 0
  \end{cases}
  \label{eq:positive_part}
\end{equation}
For nonnegative integer $m$, the finite geometric sum is
\begin{equation}
  \sum_{j=0}^{m} r^j = \frac{1-r^{m+1}}{1-r}, \qquad r\neq 1.
  \label{eq:geometric_sum}
\end{equation}
Finally, the fundamental theorem of calculus relates position and velocity.
\begin{align}
  s(t_1)-s(t_0) &= \int_{t_0}^{t_1}v(t)\,dt \\
    &= v_0(t_1-t_0)+\frac{a}{2}(t_1^2-t_0^2).
  \label{eq:displacement}
\end{align}
