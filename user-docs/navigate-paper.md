---

1. Open **PDF** (`⌥⌘P`). Selecting a Library card highlights its location in the paper.
2. Click an equation or figure in the PDF to select its Library card or Source range.
3. Hover until its grab handle appears, then drag the compiled artwork into your slide.

- The outline filters equations across included files; labels are separate manual groups.
- Custom variants navigate to their original source. New equations have no paper location.
- PDF drags use the displayed document's compiled snapshot and [export settings](export.md), in both Library and Source.

## Refresh and troubleshoot

- After external edits, **Rescan**, then **Rebuild PDF** (`⇧⌘R`). Rebuild PDF updates the document only.
- Failed builds retain the previous PDF and navigation snapshot.
- LaTeX navigation requires SyncTeX. Ambiguous or generated locations may not support direct dragging; use a Library card or Source preview.
