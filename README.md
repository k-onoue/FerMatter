Bring equations and diagrams from LaTeX and Typst papers into your presentations.

- **Extract & Browse:** Automatically collect equations and TikZ/CeTZ diagrams from your paper.
- **Presentation Variants:** Customize size, colors, and styles for slides without touching manuscript files.
- **Seamless Export:** Drag or copy directly into Keynote, PowerPoint, or export to SVG, PDF, PNG, and JPEG.
- **PDF & Source Sync:** Bi-directional navigation between compiled PDF and editor source via SyncTeX.
- **Local Automation:** Built-in stdio MCP server and CLI for agent workflows.
