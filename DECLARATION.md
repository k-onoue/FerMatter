# Declaration

## Open source

FerMatter’s [source code](https://github.com/k-onoue/FerMatter) is available under the [Apache License 2.0](LICENSE).

## No automatic data collection

FerMatter runs on your computer. The app does not automatically send your documents, equations, settings, or usage data to us.

## Free, now and in the future

FerMatter will remain free to use. We will never introduce subscriptions, paywalled features, or usage limits tied to payment.

We just look forward to seeing handmade slides and posters for your research presentations!
